Marina Bay Sands

Marina Bay Sands is one of Singapore's most famous landmarks. It is known for its three towers and rooftop SkyPark overlooking Marina Bay.

Merlion Park

Merlion Park is home to Singapore's famous Merlion statue. The Merlion has the head of a lion and the body of a fish.

Gardens by the Bay

Gardens by the Bay is a large nature park famous for its futuristic Supertrees, beautiful gardens and colourful light displays.
