Plan Your Trip

Singapore is an easy city to explore, but knowing a few useful tips before you arrive can help you make the most of your trip.

01

Best Time to Visit

Singapore has a tropical climate, so it is hot and humid throughout the year. February to April is generally a popular period to visit because there is usually less rainfall. June to August can also be busy with events and school holidays.

Pack light clothing, sunscreen and an umbrella because rain showers can happen throughout the year.

02

Getting Around

Singapore has a clean, reliable and affordable public transport system. The MRT is one of the easiest ways to travel between major attractions and neighbourhoods.

Buses are also useful for shorter journeys, while walking is a great way to explore areas such as Chinatown and Little India. Taxis and ride-hailing services are available when you need a more direct journey.

03

Currency & Payments

The official currency in Singapore is the Singapore Dollar (SGD). Credit and debit cards are widely accepted in hotels, restaurants and shopping centres.

Contactless payments are also commonly used throughout the city. It is still a good idea to carry some cash, especially when visiting smaller shops and hawker centres.

04

What to Pack

Singapore is warm and humid, so lightweight and breathable clothing is useful. Comfortable shoes are also important if you plan to explore the city on foot.

It is also useful to bring sunscreen, a reusable water bottle and a small umbrella for unexpected rain showers.
