Website Testing – Peer Assessment Checklist
Instruction: Highlight in YELLOW any points that meet the standard. Leave
unhighlighted if it needs improvement.

�� Feedback Tips:
 Be specific – say exactly what is good and what needs improvement.
 Use “I” statements – e.g., I found it easy to read the text because…
 Always write one positive and one suggestion for each section.

1. Aesthetics – How does it look?
Contrast – Text is clear and easy to read.
Alignment – Everything is lined up neatly.
Proximity – Related items are close; unrelated items have space.
Consistency – Same fonts, colours, and styles across pages.
Balance – Not too crowded or too empty.
White Space – Enough empty space around text and images.
Score (0–6): 4
Feedback:
I found the website easy to read and I thought everything was lined up neatly. 
The text was clear and the layout was simple to understand.
Since this was Sprint 1, I understand that the website was intentionally kept simple, 
but I think adding some colour and a more interesting font would make it more visually appealing.

I also noticed that there was quite a lot of white space next to the images. I think the 
images could be made bigger or the spacing could be adjusted. I also think there could be a little 
more space between some of the sections so they don't feel too close together.

___________________________________________________________

2. Functionality – Does it work?
Links – All links work and go to the right place.
Navigation – Menus and buttons take you where expected.
Scrolling – You can scroll without problems.
Page Load – No errors; all images and text appear.
Responsive – Works well on a desktop screen.
Score (0–5): 4
Feedback:  
I found that the website worked well when I used it. I was able to scroll 
through the homepage without any problems, and the images and text loaded correctly. 
The navigation links I tested also worked as expected, and the website displayed properly 
on a desktop screen.

I didn't notice any major functionality problems that needed to be fixed at this stage.
As the website develops, I would still recommend testing the new pages and links 
that are added to make sure everything continues to work correctly.
___________________________________________________________

3. Usability – Is it easy to use?
Readability – Text is big enough and easy to read.
Accessibility – Clear headings, alt text for images, good contrast.
Find Info – Information is easy to find.
User Friendly – Feels welcoming and clear.
Score (0–4): 3

Feedback:
I found the website easy to read and I thought the text was a good size. 
The headings made it easy for me to understand what each section was about, 
and the images had clear descriptions. Because the homepage was quite simple at this stage, 
I could easily find the information that was there.
I think the text could be slightly bigger in some areas to make it even easier to read. 
I also think the website could feel more welcoming if it had more colour and a more interesting font. 
Since this was only Sprint 1, I think adding more design elements and content
in the next sprint would make it more engaging.
___________________________________________________________

4. Bonus Criteria (Optional)
Purpose – Easy to see what the site is about.
Engaging – Makes you want to explore more.
Spelling – No big spelling or grammar mistakes.
Score (0–3): 2
Feedback: 
I could understand that the website was about Singapore because of the headings and the content 
that was already there. I also didn't notice any spelling or grammar mistakes, so the text was clear.
However, I didn't feel that there was a lot that made me want to explore the website yet. 
The homepage was quite basic because it mainly focused on the layout rather than the final content. 
I think adding more information underneath the headings, along with more images and colour, 
would make me more interested in exploring the website.
