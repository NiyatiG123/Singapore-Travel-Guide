but I think adding some colour and a more interesting font would make it more visually appealing.
I also noticed that there was quite a lot of white space next to the images. I think the 
images could be made bigger or the spacing could be adjusted. I also think there could be a little 
more space between some of the sections so they don't feel too close together.
