Singapore Food

Discover some of Singapore's most popular and delicious foods.

← Back to Home

Chicken Rice

Chicken rice is one of Singapore's most well-known dishes. It is made with tender chicken served with fragrant rice, usually accompanied by chilli sauce and other sauces.

Laksa

Laksa is a spicy noodle soup made with a rich, flavourful broth. It often contains noodles, seafood, chicken or tofu and is known for its strong combination of spices and coconut.

Chilli Crab

Chilli crab is a popular Singaporean seafood dish made with crab cooked in a thick, sweet and spicy sauce. It is often enjoyed with bread to soak up the sauce.

Satay

Satay consists of skewered and grilled meat served with peanut sauce. It is a popular street food and can commonly be enjoyed at hawker centres and food markets.

Kaya Toast

Kaya toast is a popular breakfast and snack consisting of toasted bread spread with kaya, a sweet coconut and egg jam. It is often served with soft-boiled eggs and a drink.
