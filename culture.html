Chinese New Year

Chinese New Year is an important celebration in Singapore. Families come together to enjoy traditional food, exchange gifts and celebrate with colourful decorations and festivities.

Hari Raya

Hari Raya Aidilfitri marks the end of Ramadan. Families and friends gather together, wear traditional clothing, visit relatives and enjoy a variety of traditional foods.
